I am a Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering at **The University of Texas at Austin**, where I work with Professor **David Fridovich-Keil** in the [Control & Learning for Autonomous Robotics (CLeAR) Lab](https://clearoboticslab.github.io/). I joined UT Austin in August 2023 and expect to graduate in **May 2028**.

My research combines **multi-agent systems, optimization, game theory, and learning**. My Ph.D. work focuses on **Games of Ordered Preference (GOOP)**, which model strategic interactions among agents with hierarchical objectives. These models describe situations where agents have conflicting goals and must give some objectives priority over others.

I focus on on making these games practical to solve for **real-time receding-horizon control**. I develop compact reformulations that remove unnecessary variables and constraints, and build a customized Newton solver for these reformulations. My goal is to reduce the computational burden of real-time planning and control for interacting agents.

My technical toolkit includes **Python, Julia, C/C++, MATLAB, and Gurobi**. I am fluent in English and a native Korean speaker.

## Background

Before UT Austin, I was a research assistant at the **Strategic Aerospace Initiative, KAIST** (September 2020–July 2023), working with Professor Jaemyung Ahn on reinforcement learning for multi-UAV routing and deep learning for aerodynamic prediction. I earned my **M.S. in Aerospace Engineering in August 2022** and **B.S. in Aerospace Engineering in February 2017**, both at KAIST. I graduated Magna cum laude with my B.S.

From June 2017 to May 2020, I served as a **First Lieutenant and Research Officer at the Agency for Defense Development** in South Korea. My work focused on multi-UAV mission planning and simulation. Earlier, I worked on a self-configuring modular drone as an undergraduate research intern in KAIST’s Unmanned Systems Research Group.
