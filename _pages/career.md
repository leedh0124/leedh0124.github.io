## The University of Texas at Austin

**Research Assistant**
August 2023–present · Austin, Texas · Advisor: David Fridovich-Keil

- Developing a **Newton solver** for real-time receding-horizon control of multiple robots with ordered objectives.
- Developed **Games of Ordered Preference (GOOP)** to model strategic agents with strict objective hierarchies; published in *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters* (2025).
- Derived a **compact reformulation of the GOOP optimality system**, improving computational performance through the formulation itself. The 2026 preprint is under review at *SIAM Journal on Optimization*.
- Co-developed methods for **mixed Nash/Stackelberg games**; the team evaluated the solver in simulation and on three ground robots with receding-horizon planning (WAFR 2026).
- Developed a convex reformulation and branch-and-bound method for **game-theoretic hierarchical routing** (*IEEE Control Systems Letters*, 2025).
- Contributed to research on approximate GOOP solutions (ITSC 2025) and strategic information gathering in noncooperative games (AAMAS 2025).
